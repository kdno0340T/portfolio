# Reference build spec (runtime capture review)

Evidence reviewed: `reference-capture/desktop-1440x900/home/full-page.png`, desktop home scroll frames at 0, 800, 4,000, 8,000, 12,000 and 16,000 px, the corresponding mobile full-page capture, desktop menu frames at 0/120/280/600/1000 ms, `desktop-1440x900/home/styles-top.json`, route capture JSON and the 151.56 s WebM sampled at 2, 8, 16 and 25 seconds. Route captures exist at both 1440x900 and 390x844.

## Observed system

- **Header and hero:** Cream/off-white textured surface; narrow fixed top row with mark at left, centered identity, and menu control at right. A large centered portrait dominates the viewport. Small editorial caption blocks sit at both sides. Desktop capture is image-led with the subject cropped at chest/shoulder scale; mobile stacks/crops the artwork rather than keeping the side columns. Reference text colors include ink `rgb(23, 18, 32)` and bone `rgb(244, 239, 230)`; cream surfaces are close to `#F4EFE6`. See `desktop-1440x900/home/scroll-000-y00000.png`, `mobile-390x844/home/scroll-000-y00000.png`, and `styles-top.json` roles `body`, `labels`, `navLinks`.
- **Signature/achievement sequence:** Full-width near-black section with a huge condensed title (captured `Signature Moments` at 313.344 px, weight 700, line-height 0.83, tracking -0.03em at 1440 px). Framed cards move horizontally while large low-contrast numerals and fine vertical rules give depth. See home scroll frames around 800–4,000 px and heading computed styles in `styles-top.json`.
- **Personal statement and story/foundation:** High-contrast dark, textured interstitials with a centered stack of offset photo-paper frames and oversized condensed statement behind it. The foundation feature switches to cream with deep-purple display text and editorial copy/image layout. See home frames around 8,000 px and `desktop-1440x900/foundation/full-page.png`.
- **Partnerships/stack:** Dark purple textured panel, extremely large condensed display title and a featured image/copy block, with a low-contrast horizontal logo band. For Dhanush, use actual tools only, no client/logo claims. See home frames around 12,000 px and `desktop-1440x900/partnerships/full-page.png`.
- **Gallery and closing:** Framed photos are arranged in a horizontal rail with captions; scroll-linked movement is visible in the long home capture. Closing panel returns to dark textured surface with oversized centered statement and a large portrait/art element. See home frames around 12,000–16,000 px and the final scroll frames.
- **Navigation:** Full-screen ink-purple (`rgb(23, 18, 32)`) overlay. Large condensed links form a centered multi-column desktop grid and a vertical mobile list. Captured link delays step by 45 ms from 80 to 350 ms; overlay opacity/visibility transition is 500 ms. See `menu-00-0ms.png` through `menu-04-1000ms.png` and `menu-animation.json`.
- **Type and layout:** The reference’s proprietary `beachwood-variable` display face is not reused. Local condensed display, compact body, and mono label fonts provide equivalents. At 1440 px the main content uses roughly 48 px side margins; body copy is about 14–15 px; labels are mono uppercase with wide tracking. Large headings reach 313 px for the signature panel and 144 px in foundation/closing content. Mobile captures use a single-column flow, smaller fluid type, and native horizontal galleries.
- **Motion and limits:** Runtime capture hints at GSAP/ScrollTrigger, Lenis-like scroll syncing, Lottie, and a Three.js render surface; observed sections use parallax, pinned horizontal sequences, and staggered menu transitions. The captures do not expose enough Three.js internals to identify its actual shader/displacement values. We use a lightweight original cutout/parallax treatment instead. Mobile and reduced-motion use ordinary document flow and horizontal overflow; no pinning.

All evidence above is a visual/runtime observation from the named capture files; details not captured (especially the 3D shader and exact font licensing) remain unknown.

## 2B implementation notes from visual review

The supplied desktop scroll video was reviewed by extracting frames at five-second intervals to `.qa/reference-video-frames/frame-000.png` through `frame-150.png`. This is a sampled frame review of the complete 151-second recording, not a source-level animation trace. The timeline below describes the recording, including its long asset wait, and must not be mistaken for intrinsic animation durations:

- `00:00–00:20`: dark ink loader remains at 0% while the page waits on assets (`frame-005.png` through `frame-020.png`). It reaches 39% near `frame-025.png`, then 99% at `frame-030.png`.
- `00:30–00:45`: a diagonal cream reveal exposes the centered portrait hero, header, side editorial notes and handwritten mark (`frame-030.png`, `frame-035.png`, `frame-040.png`). The hero settles before the next section enters.
- `00:45–01:20`: dark, pinned horizontal achievement sequence. Large background title, slim vertical grid lines, low-contrast decade markers, and portrait-format cards travel horizontally; each card has a short mono caption (`frame-050.png` through `frame-075.png`).
- `01:20–01:35`: full-screen dark statement. The condensed headline is behind an offset stack of cream photo frames; one photograph moves into the center as the headline's clip/opacity changes (`frame-080.png` through `frame-095.png`).
- `01:35–02:00`: dark partnerships feature with oversized section name, left-side copy/navigation dots, a large product visual, a view-all control, and a low-contrast continuous logo band (`frame-095.png` through `frame-120.png`).
- `02:00–02:10`: diagonal transition into the cream community/foundation panel. Large condensed statement sits behind a horizontal photo rail, followed by a small title, copy and pill CTA (`frame-120.png`, `frame-125.png`).
- `02:10–02:30`: closing brand panel, oversized text and a large cutout portrait/product composition. The slide-in navigation remains open over the left side in the final frames (`frame-130.png` through `frame-150.png`).

At 1440×900 the hero portrait fills most of the central width and is vertically cropped at the lower edge; side notes occupy narrow columns close to the margins. The signature heading spans nearly the full 1440px canvas and is around 313px. Textured cream is near `#F4EFE6`, ink surfaces near `#171220`, main cream text near `#F4EFE6`, and violet is used as a restrained detail. The screenshot's small header and copy are body sans; all-caps captions use a tracked mono. Exact values for intermediate animation frames, the shader, and internal 3D scene were not exposed by the capture. The implementation therefore uses a locally bundled condensed display face, locally bundled body and mono faces, and a lightweight CSS cutout/parallax effect in place of an unverifiable Three.js shader.

Current visual comparison baseline was captured under `.qa/mission-2b/`. Its main visible gaps were a vertically stacked project section where the reference pins horizontal cards, a dark photo gallery instead of a cream paper gallery, and no prominent signature title. The current pass replaces the home project stack with a pinned horizontal card rail and uses the cream texture for the gallery. These are visual comparisons of our content with the reference's design language; the reference photos, partner marks, branded details, wording, and exact animation art remain intentionally original or omitted.
